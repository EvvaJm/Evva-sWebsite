ClearCall

A simpler way for coaches to communicate with players.

ClearCall is a wearable communication app designed to provide a more accessible alternative to expensive, specialized communication systems used in competitive baseball and softball teams. The project connects an Android phone to a Google Pixel Watch over Bluetooth, allowing a coach to send signals from a sideline device directly to a player wearing the watch on the field. The watch provides both vibrations and on-screen messages, allowing the player to receive instructions quickly during a game.

I developed the project with softball in mind, exploring how wearable technology can improve communication between pitchers, catchers, and coaches in game. Using Android Studio and AI assistant development tools, I worked through the challenges of implementing Bluetooth communication and connecting the phone and watch components. The project gave me hands-on experience with mobile development, device connectivity, and designing technology around the practical needs of its users.

Dynamic Practice Planner

Making every minute of practice count.

The Dynamic Practice Planner is an app concept designed to help coaches manage practice schedules and drills when things don't always run according to plan. Coaches can enter drill names, plan durations, minimum and maximum time limits, and importance of each drill.

When a drill finishes earlier or later than expected, the planner automatically redistributes the difference across the remaining drills, taking their relative importance into account. This helps coaches adapt to real-world changes while keeping their practices organized and prioritizing what matters most.

I developed the core scheduling and logic of this in C and then have built the rest of the project using AI-assisted development. The concept is currently being tested by two coaches, giving me an opportunity to evaluate the logic against real-world coaching workflows and improve the experience based on user feedback.

Beanz Recovery Tracker

Keeping everyone on the same page during recovery.

The Beanz Recovery Tracker is a recovery care tracking app inspired by the challenges of caring for my dog recovering from a spinal injury, IVDD. When our dog, Bean, was requiring lots of care, it was hard for all the family members to coordinate medication, meals, bathroom breaks, and other daily tasks.

I wanted to create a simple way for everyone to record updates and see what's already been done throughout the day. The app uses a shared tracking approach with Firebase helping synchronize information across multiple family members' phones.

I built the interface using HTML and CSS, exploring how backend services could support shared data and a more connected user experience. This project combines my interest in software development with a personal motivation for my dog, Bean, making a stressful caregiving process more organized, consistent, and manageable for my family.
